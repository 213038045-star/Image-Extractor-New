About this tool

Image to Office turns a photo or scan of a document into an editable Word or Excel file. It was built by Abdur Rauf to save the time spent retyping tables and forms by hand.

What it does

  • Reads the text in your image with OCR.
  • Detects tables and rebuilds them as real Excel cells and Word tables.
  • Cuts out signatures, stamps and logos and puts them back in place.
  • Gives you plain text when the image has no table.

Limits

Handwriting, blurry photos and very small print may be read wrongly. Always check numbers and names in the result. Each image can be up to 10 MB.
