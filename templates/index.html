How it works

Three steps, usually 10–30 seconds from upload to download.

  1. Upload your image

    Drop a photo or scan into the box. Straight, well-lit images give the best result.

  2. Choose the output

    Pick Excel, Word, or both, and decide whether to match colors and extract stamps and logos.

  3. Download and check

    Your file downloads automatically. Choosing both formats gives you one .zip with the two files.

What the converter handles

Built for scanned forms, tables, reports and letters.

Real tables

Rows and columns become Excel cells and Word table cells, not a picture of a table.

Editable text

Paragraphs are read with OCR and placed in the Word file so you can edit them directly.

Signatures, stamps, logos

These are cut out of the image and placed back in the same position in the output file.

Original colors

Cell fills and text colors can be matched to the source image.

For the best result

  • Take the photo straight on, with even light and no shadows across the page.
  • Use a sharp image. Blurry text and handwriting may be read wrongly, so always check the result.
  • Keep each image under 10 MB. Convert one page at a time.

Questions

Short answers about files, accuracy and privacy.

Which image formats can I upload?

PNG, JPG, JPEG, BMP, TIF, TIFF and WEBP, up to 10 MB each.

Why did I get a .zip file?

You chose both Excel and Word. They are packed into one .zip so you can download them together. Choose only one format to get that file directly.

Is the text always correct?

No. OCR can misread blurry text, small print and handwriting. Check numbers and names before you use the file.

Is my image stored?

No. The image is deleted from the server as soon as the conversion finishes.

Why does it say the converter is busy?

The converter handles one image at a time. Wait a few seconds and press Convert again.
