Privacy

Your documents can contain private information, so this tool keeps as little as possible.

Your images

The image you upload is saved in a temporary folder only while it is being converted. It is deleted from the server as soon as the conversion finishes, whether it succeeds or fails.

Your results

The Word or Excel file is sent straight to your browser as a download. The server does not keep a copy.

Accounts and tracking

There are no accounts and no sign-up. This site does not add its own tracking. Fonts and Bootstrap are loaded from public CDNs (Google Fonts and jsDelivr).

Good practice

Avoid uploading documents with passwords, bank details or other highly sensitive data.
